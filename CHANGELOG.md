# Changelog

## v1.0 - first public release

- One-click `setup.bat`: Wi-Fi discovery, backup, debloat, launcher, tuning
- `restore.bat`: one-click undo from the backup
- Brand auto-detection; safe core list works on every Android TV
- Xiaomi / Redmi list (PatchWall and friends), tested on a Mi TV 4A (Android 9)
- Templates for TCL, Sony, Hisense, Philips, OnePlus, Google TV, Nokia
- Launcher (470 KB) with a weather screensaver that follows real weather and time of day
- `inspect.ps1` to build your own brand list
- adb included; nothing to download
